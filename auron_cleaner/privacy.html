Privacy Policy

App: Auron Cleaner

Developer: Auron Cleaner

Contact: zhengjundangads@gmail.com

Effective Date: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Auron Cleaner handles information when you use the app. We aim to handle only information necessary to provide and improve the app's functions.

1. Information and Permissions

Auron Cleaner may request access to files, photos, videos, or app-related information on your device so that it can identify items such as large files, duplicate files, similar photos, screenshots, and applications that you choose to manage. The information accessed depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant.

File and media scanning is intended to take place on your device. Auron Cleaner does not upload the contents of your personal files, photos, or videos to our servers as part of the ordinary cleaning process.

2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by the app is used to find and display items for your review, provide storage and app management features, operate and improve app performance, and respond to support requests that you choose to send us.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share the contents of your personal files, photos, or videos with third parties as part of the ordinary cleaning process. Information may be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect users, our rights, or the security of the app.

4. Data Retention and Security

Files and media identified by scanning remain on your device unless you choose to delete or manage them. We do not retain copies of those files on our servers as part of the ordinary cleaning process. We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app.

5. Your Choices

You can manage app permissions through your device settings. You can review the items identified by the app before choosing whether to delete or manage them. Please check selected items carefully before confirming deletion.

6. Children's Privacy

Auron Cleaner is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the matter.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at zhengjundangads@gmail.com.
