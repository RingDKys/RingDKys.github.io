What this notice covers

This notice explains how the developer of Footprint Walk On ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in the Footprint Walk On mobile application (the "App") and when you contact us for help. It does not govern services operated by Apple or another independent company.

Information needed to play

The App is designed for a self-contained path-and-step game experience. It does not ask you to create a profile or provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone, camera, health information, or payment-card details to play.

It may store gameplay details on your device, including the route or stage you reached, a current puzzle state, chosen sounds, and other settings. That local data helps the App continue from where you stopped. It is not transmitted to us during ordinary play.

When you write to us

Sending a support email is optional. If you do, we receive your email address and the information you include, which may contain device information, screenshots, or a description of an issue. We use that material to reply, investigate and resolve the issue, maintain the App, and keep it secure. Please avoid sending sensitive information unless it is necessary to your request.

Apple, backup, and purchases

Apple processes information related to App Store downloads, purchases, receipts, and some device services. If you enable iCloud backup, your device may include App data in that backup. Those processes are controlled by Apple and your device settings; Apple’s own terms and privacy notice apply. We do not receive your Apple ID or complete payment-card number.

No cross-app tracking in the current release

The current App is not intended to use third-party advertising networks or an advertising identifier to track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We do not use local route or puzzle data to create an advertising profile. We will update this notice before making a material change to these practices, and will request permission where required.

Limited disclosure

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may disclose limited information only when reasonably necessary to comply with law or a valid legal process, investigate misuse, protect rights, safety, security, or property, or complete a business transfer such as a merger or sale. We may also use information that has been aggregated or de-identified so that it does not reasonably identify you.

How long information remains

Local game information stays on your device until you remove it, delete the App, or use an available reset control. Your backup settings may retain a copy under Apple’s processes. Support correspondence is retained only for the period reasonably needed to respond, keep appropriate records, resolve a dispute, or meet legal obligations.

Security and children

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the small amount of information we handle, but no digital system is completely secure. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children during ordinary gameplay. If you believe a child sent us personal information through support without appropriate permission, contact us so we can review the report.

Your controls

You can remove local game data by deleting the App or using available reset controls, and can manage device permissions and iCloud backup in device settings. Depending on your location, you may be able to ask for access to, correction of, deletion of, or restrictions on personal information we hold. Email us to make a request; we may need information to verify and process it.

Changes to this notice. If the App or applicable requirements change, we may revise this notice and display a new update date. Material changes will receive additional notice where required by law.
